04 Our process Five steps

How an occasion
comes together.

From the first walk around your venue to the moment you step in as a guest at your own event.

01

Site Mapping

Every venue has its own strengths and weaknesses. Visiting the venue is essential and helps us better visualise and integrate the natural environment into our design and execution.

Also, plan that grand entrance you always wanted. *wink wink*

02

First Cut

We consider the nuances of an occasion and sketch out an initial blueprint – the requirements of a corporate gala dinner are dramatically different from anniversary gatherings. The layout is discussed and modified to suit you better – cost estimates* included.

*Still more accurate than the weather.

03

Master Plan

Celebrations are times when people come together. The entire venue is laid out to be interactive, ensuring that every guest has a wholesome experience and is within reach of all the excitement – whether old or young.

Don't fret, it will be completely stiletto- and mojari-friendly.

04

Money Wise

We believe in transparent dealings so you know exactly where your money is going. Everything you may require is listed, approximated, and discussed before execution. There is no budget too big or too small as the event is tailored according to your specific needs.

However, remember that the best things in life aren't free :P

05

Full-speed Ahead

It's D-Day. All you have to do is show up. Rest assured, we will coordinate every element to perfection. And, of course, be present through the entire event.

No really. Just show up.
