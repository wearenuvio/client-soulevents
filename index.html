04 Our process

How an occasion
comes together.

01

Site Mapping

Every venue has its strengths and weaknesses — we walk yours, then design around them.

Also, plan that grand entrance you always wanted. *wink wink*

02

First Cut

We sketch an initial blueprint for your occasion and shape it to suit you — cost estimates* included.

*Still more accurate than the weather.

03

Master Plan

The whole venue is laid out to be interactive, so every guest is within reach of the excitement.

Don't fret, it will be completely stiletto- and mojari-friendly.

04

Money Wise

Everything is listed, approximated and discussed before execution — no budget too big or too small.

However, remember that the best things in life aren't free :P

05

Full-speed Ahead

It's D-Day. We coordinate every element to perfection and stay through the whole event.

No really. Just show up.
